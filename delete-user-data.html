⚡ Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

Parents can immediately delete all account information, learning progress, and cloud database records from inside the app without waiting:

1

Open SmartAlphaX on your Android device

Launch the app and open the Navigation Drawer or tap the Settings gear icon.

2

Pass the Parent Gate

Solve the quick parental arithmetic challenge designed to prevent accidental child actions.

3

Navigate to Account / Data Management

Scroll to the Danger Zone section in Settings or Profile.

4

Tap "Delete User Data & Account"

Confirm the deletion prompt. Your Firebase account, Realtime Database records, stars, coins, and local SQLite data are permanently erased in real time.
