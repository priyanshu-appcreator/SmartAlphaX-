Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

Does SmartAlphaX require an active internet connection?
No! All core learning features—including the English alphabet, Hindi Varnamala, audio phonics, stroke tracing, and math quizzes—function completely offline. You can use SmartAlphaX during airplane travel, long drives, or areas with low connectivity.
Are advertisements safe and appropriate for young children?
Yes, absolutely. The app complies strictly with Google Play Families Policy and COPPA. All advertising is served in non-personalized mode, restricted to G-rated family content, and never tracks or profiles your child. The Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) has been explicitly purged from the app.
What is the Parent Gate?
The Parent Gate is an adult verification step (requiring the solution of a math puzzle) that protects sensitive app areas, such as the Settings menu, external web links, in-app reviews, and data reset buttons, preventing young children from accidentally altering settings.
How can I delete our account and all stored data?
You can delete everything instantly from inside the app by going to Settings > Danger Zone > "Delete User Data & Account". You may also visit our dedicated Delete User Data Web Page or email our Grievance Officer at android7392@protonmail.com.
What ages is this application suitable for?
SmartAlphaX is tailored for learners aged 3 to 10+ (preschool through primary grades). The modules adapt from early alphabet recognition and letter tracing to advanced speed multiplication and speed arithmetic.
