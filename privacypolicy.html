1. Introduction

SmartAlphaX — Learning App ("SmartAlphaX", "we", "our", or "us") is an educational Android application designed specifically for children and families.

This Privacy Policy explains what information SmartAlphaX may collect, how it is used, where it may be stored, and the choices available to parents and guardians.

We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the educational features of the application.

2. Children's Privacy

🛡️ Designed for Children

SmartAlphaX is specifically designed for children under 13 and their families.

We take children's privacy seriously and intend to follow applicable children's privacy requirements, Google Play Families policies, and applicable Indian data-protection requirements.

Where applicable law requires verifiable parental or guardian consent before processing a child's personal data, we will obtain or require such consent in an appropriate manner.

3. Information We May Collect

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if voluntarily provided
  • Profile photo, if voluntarily provided
  • Class or grade

Learning Information

  • Quiz results
  • Learning progress
  • Achievements
  • Educational statistics
  • Game or activity progress

Technical Information

Limited technical information may be processed when necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the application.

Important: SmartAlphaX does not intentionally collect or transmit precise location information or the Android Advertising ID from child users.

4. Firebase Services

Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication is used to provide account authentication through email and password.

Firebase Realtime Database

The Realtime Database may store information necessary for application functionality, including profiles, learning progress, quiz information, achievements, and related educational data.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics may be used to understand general application usage and improve application functionality.

Firebase services are intended to be configured and used in accordance with applicable children's privacy requirements and Google Play Families policies.

5. Advertising

SmartAlphaX uses Google AdMob for advertising.

  • Banner advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements
  • Rewarded advertisements

Because SmartAlphaX is directed to children, advertising must be configured for child-directed treatment and must comply with applicable Google Play Families advertising requirements.

We do not intend to use personalized or interest-based advertising for children.

Advertisements shown to children should be age-appropriate and must not contain inappropriate adult, gambling, tobacco, alcohol, sexually suggestive, or otherwise unsuitable content.

6. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

  • Precise GPS location
  • Android Advertising ID from child users
  • IMEI
  • IMSI
  • SIM serial number
  • MAC address
  • BSSID
  • SSID
  • Build serial number

We do not use SmartAlphaX to track children's physical location.

7. Educational Videos

SmartAlphaX may provide educational video content through YouTube or other video services.

When external video services are used, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Text-to-Speech and Audio

SmartAlphaX may provide pronunciation and educational audio features using text-to-speech technology.

Text used for pronunciation is processed only as necessary to provide the requested educational functionality.

9. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Save educational progress
  • Provide quizzes and games
  • Record quiz results and achievements
  • Provide educational content
  • Maintain application security
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve application performance
  • Provide appropriate advertising where permitted

We do not sell children's personal information.

We do not use children's information for targeted behavioral advertising.

10. Data Sharing

We may share or transmit information with service providers necessary to operate SmartAlphaX, including Google services such as Firebase and AdMob.

We do not sell personal information.

We do not intentionally share children's personal information with unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Parents' and Guardians' Rights

Parents and guardians may contact us regarding their child's information and may request, where applicable:

  • Access to personal information
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of personal information
  • Withdrawal of consent where applicable
  • Information about data processing

We may request sufficient information to verify that a request is being made by the child's parent or lawful guardian.

14. International Services

Some service providers used by SmartAlphaX, including Google services, may process information on servers located outside India.

Where applicable, such processing will be subject to the service provider's privacy practices and applicable legal requirements.

15. Indian Privacy Law

SmartAlphaX is intended to comply with applicable Indian privacy and data-protection requirements, including applicable requirements under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as applicable to the service.

We may update our data practices when applicable laws, platform requirements, or application functionality change.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When material changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.
