02

Children's Privacy & Safety

Privacy protections for young learners

SmartAlphaX is designed for young learners, including preschool and primary-school children. We aim to minimize the collection and processing of children's personal information and to comply with applicable child privacy laws and platform requirements.

Drawing and tracing activities

Drawing and tracing strokes are processed on the device for educational interaction. Under the intended local-learning design, these strokes are not uploaded to our servers.

Voice and microphone

The App may provide text-to-speech (TTS) pronunciation for letters and numbers. Text-to-speech is different from recording a child's voice. The App is not intended to record or upload a child's voice for these pronunciation activities.

Camera and facial recognition

The App's described learning experience does not require facial recognition. Child avatars may use pre-rendered cartoon illustrations or emojis. Any future camera-related feature would require an appropriate review and an updated disclosure.

Social features

SmartAlphaX is intended for individual learning rather than public social networking. We do not provide public forums or open child-to-child chat as part of the described core learning experience.

Parents and guardians: Please supervise children's use of online features and contact us if you believe that personal information has been collected inappropriately.

03

Information We Collect & Lawful Basis

Local storage, account information and cloud sync

A. Local on-device storage

SmartAlphaX may use the Android Room persistence library, backed by SQLite, to store educational information on the device.

Depending on the features used, local data may include:

  • Completed alphabet and phonics activities.
  • Quiz scores and educational progress.
  • Math tables and completed learning activities.
  • Stars, coins, streak counts and other learning indicators.
  • Locally saved learning preferences or child profiles, where implemented.

Locally stored information may remain on the device until it is cleared within the App, removed through an available reset function, or deleted through applicable device-level actions.

B. Optional parent account and Firebase

If account creation and cloud synchronization are enabled and used, the following information may be processed:

  • Email address: used for account authentication.
  • Firebase user ID (UID): used to associate an authenticated account with its cloud records.
  • Educational progress: information synchronized to the account's associated database records.

Firebase Authentication handles password authentication. The App is not intended to store a user's password as readable plaintext in its educational database.

Cloud progress is intended to be stored under the authenticated user's database path, such as /users/{uid}/, where that structure is implemented.

Firebase Realtime Database security rules must be configured and tested to restrict each user to the records they are authorized to access.

C. Purposes of processing

  • Providing educational activities and progress tracking.
  • Authenticating parent accounts, where enabled.
  • Synchronizing learning progress across supported devices.
  • Responding to support requests and privacy inquiries.
  • Maintaining security and preventing unauthorized access.

D. Consent and applicable legal requirements

Where applicable law requires verifiable parental consent or other authorization before processing a child's personal data, the required process must be completed before that processing begins.

We aim to process personal data only for specified purposes and to limit it to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant service, subject to applicable legal obligations.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and its applicable rules and commencement provisions are relevant to our privacy practices. The specific obligations applicable to a service depend on the law in force and the circumstances of the processing.

Advertising & Google Play Families

Child-directed advertising and ad disclosures

SmartAlphaX may display advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Advertising is subject to the configuration of the published app, applicable Google policies and applicable law.

Child-directed advertising

Where required, advertising requests are intended to use appropriate child-directed treatment, non-personalized advertising settings and a maximum permitted ad content rating suitable for the intended audience.

These settings may include Google's child-directed treatment and non-personalized advertising request parameters, where applicable. The actual request configuration must be verified in the released app.

Advertising identifiers

The use of advertising identifiers and related measurement technologies depends on the app's manifest, SDK dependencies, SDK behavior and applicable platform requirements.

We aim to avoid unnecessary tracking of children and to configure advertising in accordance with the requirements applicable to child-directed apps.

Ad placement

  • Advertisements should be distinguishable from educational content and interactive learning controls.
  • Ads should not mislead children into accidentally clicking them.
  • Advertising must comply with the Google Play Families Policy and applicable advertising rules.

Please note that non-personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that an advertising SDK processes no data. Data processing depends on the services used and their configuration.

Information Security Safeguards

Protecting account and learning information

We aim to implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or misuse.

Network security

Network communications with Firebase and other online services should use secure encrypted connections as supported and required by the relevant service and platform.

Authentication

Account authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication where that service is enabled. Authentication credentials and tokens should be handled according to the relevant SDK security practices.

Database access

Cloud database security rules should restrict access to authenticated users and authorize access only to the records they are permitted to access.

Parental controls

Where implemented, a parent gate or PIN can help prevent children from accidentally accessing administrative actions and settings. A parent gate is an additional safeguard, not a replacement for secure account access and backend authorization.

Limitations

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. Security measures must be maintained, tested and updated as the App and its dependencies change.
