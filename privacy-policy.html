2. Children's Privacy & Zero PII Guarantee

Our Core Child Safety Commitment:

SmartAlphaX is designed for young learners (preschoolers, toddlers, and primary school students). We do NOT knowingly collect, harvest, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) from children.

  • No Child Biometrics: Drawing and tracing strokes created on screen are rendered and processed in real time on the child's device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • No Audio or Voice Harvesting: The app includes text-to-speech (TTS) pronunciation for letters and numbers, but does NOT record, store, or transmit the child's voice.
  • No Facial or Camera Recognition: Camera hardware is never accessed. Child avatars are pre-rendered cartoon emojis (e.g., 👧, 👦, 🚀, 🦁) selected on-device.
  • No Social Feeds or Open Chat: There are no public forums, real-time messaging, or child-to-child communication channels.

3. Information We Collect & Lawful Basis

Data processing is strictly minimized and grounded in legitimate educational fulfillment:

A. Local On-Device Storage (Room SQLite Database)

All educational progress data—such as completed alphabets, quiz scores, math tables, gamification stars, coins, and streak counts—is saved strictly on the user's Android device using the local SQLite Room database. This allows the application to function completely offline without internet connectivity.

B. Optional Parent Account & Cloud Backup (Firebase)

If a parent or guardian voluntarily creates an account to sync progress across family devices:

  • Parent Credentials: Parent email address, masked password (handled securely via Google Firebase Authentication), and user ID (UID).
  • Cloud Database Path: Educational progress is mirrored under /users/{uid}/ in Google Firebase Realtime Database. Realtime Database security rules strictly restrict access so that only the authenticated parent can read or write their family's data.

C. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, our processing is based on:

  • Verifiable Parental Consent: Required during initial setup before cloud features are enabled.
  • Service Fulfillment: Fulfilling user-requested educational tracking, certificate generation, and streak continuity.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies Disclosure

SmartAlphaX does NOT use browser cookies.

Because SmartAlphaX is a native Android application built with Jetpack Compose, it does not use HTTP cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, or third-party web tracking technologies.

Any embedded legal or help documentation runs offline directly from bundled Android assets (file:///android_asset/legal/) without creating, reading, or transmitting tracking cookies.

5. Advertising & Google Play Families Compliance

SmartAlphaX adheres strictly to the Google Play Families Policy and child-directed advertising standards:

  • No Behavioral Tracking / No Personalized Ads: Advertising requests are configured with tagForChildDirectedTreatment=true, non-personalized ads (npa=1), and maximum content rating set to G (General Audiences).
  • Advertising ID Removed: The Google Advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and AdServices measurement signals have been explicitly removed from the application manifest using tools:node="remove".
  • No Deceptive Ad Placement: Advertisements never interfere with learning flows, do not mimic gameplay buttons, and are clearly demarcated.

7. Data Retention & 1-Click Account Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide learning continuity:

  • In-App 1-Click Reset: Parents can clear all on-device learning scores and child profiles instantly via the "Reset All Kid Progress" button in Settings.
  • In-App Account & Cloud Data Deletion: Parents can permanently delete their cloud Firebase account and all associated cloud progress records at any time by tapping "Delete User Data & Account" in Settings or the Profile screen.
  • Web Data Deletion Instructions: Visit our dedicated Account & User Data Deletion Page for statutory web instructions.
  • Grievance Deletion Request: Parents can also email android7392@protonmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Requests are executed within 48 business hours.

8. Parental & User Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act) and international privacy frameworks, parents hold the following rights:

  • Right to Access & Summary: Parents may request a summary of the personal data held about their account.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Parents may request correction of inaccurate data or complete erasure of their account.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Parents have the right to an accessible mechanism to resolve data protection inquiries.
  • Right to Nominate: Parents may nominate an individual to exercise their rights in case of death or incapacity.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Parents may withdraw consent at any time without punitive consequences.

9. Information Security Safeguards

  • Authentication tokens are managed through modern Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase SDK protocols.
  • A PIN-protected Parent Gate guards administrative actions, purchases, and settings against accidental modification by children.
